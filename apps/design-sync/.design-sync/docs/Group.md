# Group

A `card` with an optional title and description: Radix's step-2 fill (`bg-card`), `rounded-card`, a step-6 `border-hairline` edge, no shadow.

The standard container for a section of a form or a dashboard block. Its edge is what sets it off the step-1 page.

A group that is the only one on its page or section takes `card={false}` and is drawn as a plain section with no card; two or more groups each keep theirs.
